How we calculate

  • Strike rate = winning selections ÷ settled selections.
  • Profit/loss (units) = for each winner, (odds − 1); for each loser, −1.
  • ROI = total profit/loss ÷ total units staked.

Past results do not predict future results. A positive or negative figure over a small number of selections means very little. Betting is not a way to make money.
