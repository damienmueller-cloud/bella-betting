About Bella Betting & responsible gambling

About us

Bella Betting publishes a short, free tips card for Australian racing (and occasionally sport). Each selection comes with our reasoning, a confidence rating and the odds we saw at a stated time, so you can judge it for yourself.

Bella Betting is not a bookmaker or wagering service. We do not take bets, hold money, run competitions, or offer bonus bets, promotions or sign-up deals. We have no affiliate, referral or commission arrangement with any bookmaker. Where a bookmaker is named, it is only to show where the quoted odds came from.

Our tips are general opinion only. They are not financial advice and they do not take your personal situation into account. Most bets lose. Betting is entertainment that costs money; it is not a way to make money or fix money problems.

Responsible gambling

18+ only. This site is intended for adults. If you are under 18, please leave this site.

  • Set a budget before you start and stick to it. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
  • Never chase losses or borrow money to bet.
  • Use deposit limits and time-outs offered by your licensed provider.
  • Take regular breaks, and don’t bet when you’re upset, stressed or have been drinking.
  • Gambling should never get in the way of work, study, family or friends.

Get help

  • Gambling Help Online: free, confidential support 24/7. Call 1800 858 858 or visit gamblinghelponline.org.au.
  • BetStop – the National Self-Exclusion Register: exclude yourself from all licensed Australian online and phone wagering providers at betstop.gov.au.
  • If you are worried about someone else’s gambling, Gambling Help Online also supports family and friends.

Only use licensed Australian providers

Offshore gambling sites that are not licensed in Australia are illegal to offer here and give you none of the protections of Australian law. The ACMA publishes a list of blocked illegal gambling websites at acma.gov.au.
